Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what the game Splash Blast! ("the game", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: aliburakbarac@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Game account. On first launch the game creates an anonymous account (a random ID). No name, email or phone number is asked for. If you choose to link Apple or Google sign-in, we receive the account identifier (and the email address the provider shares, which may be a private relay address).
  • Game data. Your progress, coins, boosters, lives, rewards claimed and the in-game actions that change them, with server timestamps. This keeps your game safe and lets it continue on another device.
  • Purchases. When you buy something, the store (Apple or Google) processes the payment. We receive the transaction ID, product, price and refund status through our purchase partner RevenueCat. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Ads. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. On iPhone, personalized ads are only used if you allow tracking in the system prompt; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first. You can change these choices in Settings at any time.
  • Analytics and crash reports. Firebase Analytics (Google) counts how the game is played (for example levels started and won) and Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports with device model and OS version. These use your anonymous game ID, not your name.

Why we use it

To run the game and keep your progress, deliver purchases, prevent cheating and fraud, fix crashes, balance and improve levels, and show ads that fund the free game. Our legal bases are performing our contract with you (the game), our legitimate interests (security, improvement) and, where required, your consent (personalized ads, analytics in the EEA).

Who we share it with

Only service providers who run parts of the game for us: Supabase (game servers, EU – Frankfurt), RevenueCat (purchases), Google (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics), Apple and Google Play (stores). We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

Game and purchase data are kept while your game account exists; purchase records may be kept longer where tax law requires. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction or deletion by writing to aliburakbarac@gmail.com with your player ID (shown in Settings). Deleting your game account removes your progress and cannot be undone. You can also reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your phone's settings. If you are in the EEA, UK or Turkey you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
