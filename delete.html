Delete your Splash Blast! account

Splash Blast! (App Store and Google Play) gives every player an anonymous game account. You can delete it, with all of its data, in two ways.

In the game

Open Settings → About → Delete account and tap it twice to confirm. The account is deleted on our server at once, and the game starts over as a new player.

Without the game

Write to aliburakbarac@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and your player ID (Settings, bottom of the page). If you no longer have the game, tell us the Apple or Google account you linked, or the approximate date you last played and your phone model, and we will find it. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your game account and everything stored with it: level progress, stars, coins, boosters, lives, event progress, your personal difficulty score, your RevenueCat purchase profile, and the link to an Apple or Google sign-in (an Apple sign-in is also revoked with Apple).

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Records of purchases are kept for tax law with the link to you removed. Aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example how many players finished a level) may remain. Store receipts are kept by Apple or Google under their own policies, and your purchases can still be restored to a new account with "Restore Purchases".

Hesabını sil (Türkçe)

Oyunda: Ayarlar → Hakkında → Hesabı sil'e iki kez bas. Hesap sunucumuzdan hemen silinir ve oyun yeni bir oyuncu olarak baştan başlar.

Oyun olmadan: "Hesabımı sil" konusuyla ve oyuncu kimliğinle aliburakbarac@gmail.com adresine yaz. Oyun artık sende yoksa bağladığın Apple ya da Google hesabını veya son oynadığın tarihi ve telefon modelini yaz. Hesabı 30 gün içinde silip e-postayla haber veririz.

Silinenler: oyun hesabın ve ona bağlı her şey (ilerleme, yıldızlar, coin, güçlendiriciler, canlar, etkinlikler, kişisel zorluk puanı, satın alma profili, Apple / Google bağlantısı). Vergi mevzuatı için satın alma kayıtları seninle bağı koparılarak saklanır. Seni tanımlamayan toplu, anonim istatistikler kalabilir.
