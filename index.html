Splash. Bounce. Paint the picture.

Splash Blast! is a bright puzzle game: aim your paint balls, bank them off the walls and color every block of the picture. Hundreds of hand-made levels, boosters, a season festival, races and an album of paintings to finish with Otto.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.
